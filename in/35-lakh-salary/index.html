₹35 lakh salary: in-hand per month (FY 2026–27)

A ₹35 lakh annual salary gives about ₹2,21,317 in hand per month under the new regime in FY 2026–27 — ₹26,55,800 a year after ₹6,31,800 income tax, ₹2,10,000 employee PF and ₹2,400 professional tax. Under the old regime with ₹1.5 lakh in 80C it is ₹2,04,479 per month (₹8,33,851 tax).

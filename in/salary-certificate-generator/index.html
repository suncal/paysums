Employers only

The letter names the issuing company and the signatory and carries an attestation that the details come from HR and payroll records — verifiers phone the signatory, which is what makes the letter worth anything. Employees cannot issue these to themselves; a forged salary certificate is a criminal offence and gets loan applications blacklisted.

Employees: asking for one

Email HR: “Please issue a salary certificate for the purpose of [loan/visa/rental], stating my designation, date of joining, gross and net monthly salary and annual CTC, on company letterhead, signed by the authorised signatory.” Most HR teams turn it around in a day; the salary slip and Form 16 are the supporting documents.

Frequently asked

Is a salary certificate the same as a salary slip?

No. The slip is a monthly payroll record; the certificate is a letter confirming employment and pay, usually for a third party.

Can the experience letter mention performance?

Keep it factual: dates, designation, conduct. Avoid negative remarks; if you cannot give a positive conduct line, leave it out.

Relieving letter vs experience certificate?

The relieving letter confirms the resignation was accepted and the employee is released on a date; the experience certificate summarises the tenure. New employers often want both.

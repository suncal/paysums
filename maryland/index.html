Frequently asked

How much is $60,000 after taxes in Maryland?

About $46,193 a year, $3,849 a month or $1,777 per bi-weekly paycheck for a single filer in 2026: $5,020 federal income tax, $4,590 FICA and $2,486 Maryland state tax.

What is the Maryland state income tax rate?

Maryland taxes wages progressively from 2% to 6.5% across 10 brackets; standard deduction $3,350 single, $6,700 married.

Does Maryland have local income taxes?

Yes — Baltimore City / County 3.2%, Montgomery County 3.2%, Prince George's County 3.2%, Howard County 3.2%, Anne Arundel County 2.7-3.2%. Pick yours in the calculator.

How is federal tax calculated?

Wages minus pre-tax deductions minus the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 married) run through the seven federal brackets from 10% to 37%. Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000.

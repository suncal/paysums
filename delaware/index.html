Frequently asked

How much is $60,000 after taxes in Delaware?

About $47,737 a year, $3,978 a month or $1,836 per bi-weekly paycheck for a single filer in 2026: $5,020 federal income tax, $4,590 FICA and $2,653 Delaware state tax.

What is the Delaware state income tax rate?

Delaware taxes wages progressively from 2.2% to 6.6% across 7 brackets; standard deduction $3,250 single, $6,500 married.

Does Delaware have local income taxes?

Yes — Wilmington (1.25%). Pick yours in the calculator.

How is federal tax calculated?

Wages minus pre-tax deductions minus the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 married) run through the seven federal brackets from 10% to 37%. Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000.

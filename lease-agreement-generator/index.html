Residential lease agreement generator: your terms, standard clauses, one PDF

A lease should say what the rent is, when it is due, what happens when it is late, who pays for what, and how the tenancy ends — in language that holds up. This generator drafts a complete residential lease from your answers with the clauses landlords and tenants expect, the federal lead-paint disclosure for pre-1978 housing, and a governing-law clause for your state. Preview free; the PDF is $9.99 once. See what the rent means for the tenant with after taxes and rent by city.

This tool is for the employer, payer or individual whose own records these are. It computes the document from figures you supply and invents nothing. Frequently asked

Is this valid in every state?

The clauses are standard nationwide and defer to state law; some states require specific disclosures or forms in addition — attach them as addenda.

Can I use it for a room rental?

Yes — describe the room and shared areas in “Premises” and “Included”.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is a general template; for rent-controlled units, commercial space or unusual terms, have a local attorney review it.

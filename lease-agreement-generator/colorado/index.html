Frequently asked

Is a generic lease valid in Colorado?

Yes as a contract, as long as it does not contradict Colorado landlord-tenant law; clauses that do are unenforceable and the state's mandatory disclosures must be attached. The generator's governing-law clause defers to Colorado law for exactly that reason.

What income should I require for a $2,000 rent in Colorado?

About $107,575 gross a year for rent to be 30% of take-home in Colorado, or $6,000 a month under the 3× rule — write the rule you use into the application and apply it uniformly.

Month-to-month or fixed term?

Fixed term gives both sides certainty on rent and occupancy; month-to-month needs 30 days' notice (more in some states) and suits short stays. The generator handles either.

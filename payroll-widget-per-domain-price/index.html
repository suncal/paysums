Payroll widget per domain price

You can embed a payroll widget on your website with Paysums. The white-label plan costs $19 per month or $149 per year per domain. This plan removes the "Powered by paysums.com" line from the widget.

How it works for a single website

  1. Go to /widgets/ and copy the embed code for the payroll calculator.
  2. Paste the code into your site’s HTML where you want the calculator to appear.
  3. Choose the white-label plan ($19/mo or $149/yr) and pay the subscription.
  4. The widget loads in the visitor’s browser, runs offline, and shows take-home pay for any of the 50 states + DC.
  5. Your site displays the calculator without the “Powered by paysums.com” line.

What you get and the cost

  • White-label payroll widget – removes the attribution line.
  • Pricing – $19 per month or $149 per year per domain.
  • Features – full 2026 payroll calculations: federal, state, and covered city taxes; 401(k), HSA, health deductions; single, married filing jointly, head of household, married filing separately filing statuses.
  • No account needed – the widget works in the browser and offline.
  • Support – included with the subscription; you can also upgrade to the API Pro plan ($29/mo) for higher request limits if you need JSON data elsewhere.

FAQ

What does the white-label plan include? It gives you the payroll calculator widget without the “Powered by paysums.com” line. Theme and accent colors are the only customisation options.

Can I use the widget on more than one domain? The price listed is per domain. To add another domain you would pay the same $19/mo or $149/yr for that additional site.

Is there a free version of the widget? A free widget is available, but it always shows the “Powered by paysums.com” attribution. Removing that line requires the paid white-label plan.

To get started with the payroll widget for your domain, go to the widgets page and follow the instructions to embed the calculator on your site.

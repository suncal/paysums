Nanny payroll calculator for Colorado (2026): take-home pay, withholding and the family's cost

A full-time nanny in Colorado at $20 an hour (40 hours a week) takes home $660 a week after federal tax, Social Security and Medicare and Colorado income tax ($22 a week). The family's total cost is $44,824 a year: $41,600 in wages plus $3,182 employer Social Security and Medicare and $42 FUTA, before state unemployment insurance.

Colorado rules for household employers

  • Colorado income tax applies to the nanny’s wages; withholding follows the state’s tables ($22 a week at $20/h) and is remitted to the state.
  • Register with the Colorado unemployment insurance agency once you pay $1,000 in a quarter; the rate is set per employer.
  • Federal: W-2 by January 31, Copy A to the SSA, Schedule H with your 1040 by April 15.

Frequently asked

How much does a nanny cost in Colorado?

At $20 an hour full-time: $41,600 in wages plus about $3,224 in employer taxes = $44,824 a year, plus Colorado unemployment insurance. At $25 an hour: $56,020.

How much does a nanny take home in Colorado?

$660 a week at $20 an hour, $811 at $25, $962 at $30 — single filer, standard withholding.

Does the nanny pay tax in April too?

If income tax is withheld at the W-4 amounts, usually not; if you agreed not to withhold, she owes the year's income tax on her return and should make estimated payments.

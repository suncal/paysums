How lenders count bank-statement income

  • Personal statements: 100% of eligible deposits over 12 or 24 months, divided by the months.
  • Business statements: deposits minus an expense ratio — 50% by default, lower with a CPA letter or a P&L showing thinner margins.
  • Excluded: transfers between your own accounts, loan proceeds, refunds, gifts, and large one-off deposits without a documented source.
  • Consistency matters: declining deposits, overdrafts and NSFs lower the usable figure; recurring deposits from the same clients raise confidence.
  • Rates run 0.5–1.5 points above conventional loans and down payments of 10–20% are typical — which is why it pays to know the qualifying figure before shopping.

Frequently asked

What is a bank statement loan?

A mortgage for borrowers whose tax returns understate their cash flow (self-employed, business owners, gig workers). The lender qualifies you on deposits shown on 12–24 months of statements rather than on W-2s or returns.

Does uploading my statements share them with anyone?

No. The file is parsed by JavaScript running in your browser; nothing leaves your device. Prefer your bank's CSV export — PDF layouts vary and some are read imperfectly.

Is the proof-of-income summary a substitute for statements?

No — it is a cover sheet that makes the statements readable. It states on its face that it was prepared by the applicant from the statements listed, and it should be sent with them.

Why are transfers excluded?

Moving money from your own savings is not income; lenders strip it out, so the calculator does too unless you tick the box.

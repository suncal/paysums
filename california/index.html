California paycheck calculator 2026

California taxes wages progressively from 1% to 13.3% across 10 brackets; standard deduction $5,706 single, $11,412 married. Employees also pay CA SDI (1.3%). California's withholding tables run at 110% of these rates; the extra comes back as a refund. A $60,000 salary in California takes home about $47,970 a year in 2026 — $3,998 a month or $1,845 every two weeks for a single filer. Frequently asked

How much is $60,000 after taxes in California?

About $47,970 a year, $3,998 a month or $1,845 per bi-weekly paycheck for a single filer in 2026: $5,020 federal income tax, $4,590 FICA and $1,640 California state tax.

What is the California state income tax rate?

California taxes wages progressively from 1% to 13.3% across 10 brackets; standard deduction $5,706 single, $11,412 married. Employees also pay CA SDI (1.3%). California's withholding tables run at 110% of these rates; the extra comes back as a refund.

Does California have local income taxes?

No. Cities and counties in California do not levy their own wage taxes, so state tax is the only line below the federal ones.

How is federal tax calculated?

Wages minus pre-tax deductions minus the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 married) run through the seven federal brackets from 10% to 37%. Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000.

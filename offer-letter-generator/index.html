Frequently asked

Exempt or non-exempt?

Non-exempt employees must be paid overtime. Exempt status needs both a salary above the federal/state threshold and duties that qualify (executive, administrative, professional…). When unsure, non-exempt.

Should the letter mention a probation period?

Avoid it — it can imply job security after the period. At-will language covers both sides.

Can I add a signing bonus with clawback?

Put it in the bonus sentence with the repayment condition (e.g. repaid if you leave within 12 months); state law limits deductions from final pay, so make it a separate repayment obligation.

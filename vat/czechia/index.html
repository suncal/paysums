Frequently asked

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross amount by (1 + rate): at 21% that is ÷ 1.21. The calculator does it when you pick “gross (remove VAT)”.

Which VAT rate applies?

The standard rate (21%) unless the goods or services are on a reduced list (12%). Check the tax authority's list for food, books, medicines, energy and hospitality.

Florida paycheck calculator 2026

Florida has no state income tax on wages, so take-home pay is gross pay minus federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare. A $60,000 salary in Florida takes home about $50,390 a year in 2026 — $4,199 a month or $1,938 every two weeks for a single filer. Frequently asked

How much is $60,000 after taxes in Florida?

About $50,390 a year, $4,199 a month or $1,938 per bi-weekly paycheck for a single filer in 2026: $5,020 federal income tax, $4,590 FICA and $0 Florida state tax.

What is the Florida state income tax rate?

Florida has no state income tax on wages, so take-home pay is gross pay minus federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Does Florida have local income taxes?

No. Cities and counties in Florida do not levy their own wage taxes, so state tax is the only line below the federal ones.

How is federal tax calculated?

Wages minus pre-tax deductions minus the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 married) run through the seven federal brackets from 10% to 37%. Social Security is 6.2% up to $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% with an extra 0.9% above $200,000.

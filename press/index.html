Frequently asked

Can we embed a calculator instead of a table?

Yes — two lines of HTML from the widgets page; white-label is available for publishers.

How current are the numbers?

Tax tables are the 2026 official tables and are checked weekly; mortgage rates refresh weekly (Freddie Mac), exchange rates weekly (ECB), HUD rents yearly, Census home values yearly. The change log lists every update with its source.

Do you need credit?

A link to the page you used is the only condition (CC BY 4.0).

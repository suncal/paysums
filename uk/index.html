Frequently asked

How is UK take-home pay calculated?

Gross pay minus pension (if any) gives taxable pay. Income tax is charged on the part above the £12,570 personal allowance at 20%, 40% and 45% (19–48% in Scotland). Class 1 National Insurance is 8% of earnings between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above. Student loan repayments take 9% of pay above the plan threshold (6% for postgraduate loans).

What is the personal allowance for 2026/27?

£12,570, unchanged. It reduces by £1 for every £2 earned above £100,000 and disappears at £125,140, which creates an effective 60% rate in that band.

What are the student loan thresholds for 2026/27?

Plan 1 £26,900, Plan 2 £29,385, Plan 4 £33,795, Plan 5 £25,000 (first repayments from April 2026), Postgraduate £21,000.

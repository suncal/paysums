P45 generator for employers: the leaver's parts, matching your FPS

When an employee leaves you report the leaving date on the Full Payment Submission and must give them a P45 (Parts 1A, 2 and 3) — the new employer needs it to carry on the right tax code. Enter the leaver's details and the year-to-date figures from your payroll; the three parts print in the standard layout. $5.99; preview free.

This tool produces a substitute P45 (Parts 1A, 2 and 3) with the information HMRC requires. It is not an HMRC-printed form and does not replace the leaver information you send HMRC in your Full Payment Submission; figures must match your payroll records. Employers only. This tool is for the employer, payer or individual whose own records these are. It computes the document from figures you supply and invents nothing. Presenting a document from this site as evidence of pay you did not receive is fraud, and every figure on it must match your real payroll or payment records. See the terms of use.

Part 1 goes to HMRC electronically through your FPS — this tool does not replace that submission; it prints the employee's parts from the same figures, and says so. An employee cannot issue a P45 to themselves; a new employer checks the figures against HMRC's records through the starter process.

Lost P45?

Employers cannot issue a duplicate P45; the new employer uses the starter checklist and HMRC sends the correct code. This tool is for the original issue (or a re-print of the original figures by the same employer).

Frequently asked

Week 1 / month 1 codes?

Tick the basis; the P45 then shows this-employment pay and tax rather than cumulative totals.

Employee died?

Do not issue a P45; report the date of death on the FPS.

Director or pensioner?

Same form; pensions use a P45 when a pension stops.

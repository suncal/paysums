£24,000 after tax (2026/27)

On a £24,000 salary in 2026/27 you take home £20,800 a year — £1,733 a month or £400 a week — after £2,286 income tax and £914 National Insurance (England, Wales and Northern Ireland, tax code 1257L, no pension). In Scotland it is £20,839; with a Plan 2 student loan £20,800; with a 5% pension £19,840.

90026 sales tax rate: 9.75% (Los Angeles, CA)

ZIP code 90026 lies inside Los Angeles, CA, where the combined sales tax rate is 9.75% (7.25% state + 2.5% local). On a $100 purchase that is $9.75 in tax, $109.75 total. Addresses in 90026 that fall outside the city limits can carry a different local rate — check the official California rate lookup for a specific address.

Questions about sales tax in 90026

What is the sales tax rate in 90026?

9.75% in 2026 — 7.25% California state tax plus 2.5% county, city and district tax. On $100 that is $9.75 of tax.

How much is sales tax on $1,000 in 90026?

$97.50, for a total of $1,097.50. Multiply any price by 0.0975 for the tax, or by 1.0975 for the total.

How do I work out the price before tax from a 90026 receipt?

Divide the total by 1.0975. A $109.75 receipt was $100.00 before 9.75% sales tax. Switch the calculator above to “gross (remove sales tax)” and it does it for you.

Why does my receipt show a different rate than 9.75%?

ZIP codes are postal routes, not tax boundaries. Part of 90026 can fall outside the Los Angeles city limits or into a district with its own add-on, and some districts change rates on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July or 1 October. For a specific street address use the official California rate lookup.

Is the rate on this page current?

It is the published 2026 combined rate for Los Angeles, CA. The rate tables behind this site are checked against the state revenue departments every week and every change is listed in the change log.

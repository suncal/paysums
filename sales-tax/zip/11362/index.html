11362 sales tax rate: 8.875% (New York City, NY)

ZIP code 11362 lies inside New York City, NY, where the combined sales tax rate is 8.875% (4% state + 4.875% local). On a $100 purchase that is $8.88 in tax, $108.88 total. Addresses in 11362 that fall outside the city limits can carry a different local rate — check the official New York rate lookup for a specific address.

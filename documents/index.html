The rule behind all of them

Computed, not typed

Tax, FICA, NI, TDS and EPF come from the official tables. You cannot type a withholding number.

Labelled

What you entered is marked as entered; what was computed is marked as computed, with the year and the tables used.

Signed by the right party

Employer forms attest the employer; payer forms attest the payer. A recipient cannot make their own W-2 or 1099 here.

That is why landlords, lenders and HR teams can rely on what comes out — and why we keep a payment processor and an ad network.

Frequently asked

Which one do I need to prove income?

Employees: your employer's stubs first; if there are none, the pay stub generator plus bank statements. Contractors: invoices plus bank statements, and the 1099 contractor statement. Anyone: the verification letter signed by the payer.

How do the download keys work?

Buy once, get a key by email, paste it into the tool, print or save the PDF. A single key covers one document; the 10-pack covers ten documents on any of these tools.

Do you see my documents?

No — they are generated in your browser. Only the key is checked against our licence server.
